How to play · learning goals · save instructions

Goal: Guide Sunny the puppy through eight coves. Collect three bones in each cove to open its question. Bones give +10 points; a correct first answer gives +50. Read the explanation, then continue.

Gentle obstacles: Crabs, jellyfish, and spiky shells cost one heart and 10 points. Sunny blinks briefly after a bump. With no hearts left, a lifeguard restores three hearts and returns Sunny to the current cove's start for 20 more points. Collected bones stay collected. Scores never drop below zero.

Time: Four minutes of movement time. Questions, feedback, and pauses stop the clock. If time runs out, finish the remaining questions in learning mode. You can always complete all eight.

Keyboard: Move with arrow keys or WASD. Press P to pause. Use Tab and Enter to operate buttons; number keys 1–4 choose an answer. The on-screen arrows also work with touch or a mouse.

Learning alignment — North Carolina Health Education: 6.ANCOD.2: Apply refusal skills to protect self and others from alcohol, nicotine products, cannabis, and other drug use. Questions 1–2 practice refusal and getting help; 3–4 address 6.ANCOD.2.1 (personal responsibility); 5–6 address 6.ANCOD.2.2 (school and community support); 7–8 address 6.ANCOD.2.3 (how healthy and risky behaviors influence future behaviors).

Save and open: Copy this game's entire HTML code into a plain-text editor. Save as puppy-beach-quest.html (choose “All files” if needed), then double-click the file to open it in a modern web browser. All graphics, styles, questions, and game logic are included; no internet connection is needed.

Classroom tip: After playing, practice saying a firm “no,” suggesting a safe activity, and identifying a trusted adult. This game does not collect or send student information.
